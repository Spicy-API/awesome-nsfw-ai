# Contributing to Awesome NSFW AI

Thanks for helping keep this list useful. Additions, fixes and removals of dead links are all welcome, including tools that compete with SpicyAPI.

## What belongs here

A resource is a good fit when it:

- generates, edits, enhances or organises adult (18+) AI images, video, audio or text, or helps developers build with such models;
- is publicly available and still maintained (a commit or release in the last 12 months, or a live hosted service);
- has a clear license or terms of service.

## What does not belong here

We will close pull requests for resources whose main purpose is:

- sexual content involving minors or anyone who appears to be a minor, in any style;
- "undressing", "nudifying" or face/head-swapping real people without documented consent;
- impersonation, harassment, extortion, fake evidence or defeating identity checks;
- evading laws, payment-network rules or another platform's safety systems.

## How to add a resource

1. Search the list first to avoid duplicates.
2. Add **one resource per pull request** in the most specific section.
3. Use this format:

   ```markdown
   - [Name](https://link): one neutral sentence on what it does and who it is for.
   ```

   For table sections, fill every column. State prices only if you can link the provider's own pricing page, and add the date you checked.
4. No marketing superlatives ("best", "#1", "revolutionary") unless you link a public test that supports them.
5. Disclose your relationship if you work on or are paid by the resource.

## Updating prices and model facts

SpicyAPI model rows come from the public catalog at `https://api.spicyapi.ai/console/v1/catalog/models`. If a price or duration changed, update the row and the "updated" badge date in the README.
